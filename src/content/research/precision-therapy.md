---
title: Precision therapy for breast cancer
image: ./precision-therapy.jpg
summary: Every breast cancer is a unique disease — there is no "one size fits all" therapy. In preclinical work we are examining how to use bioinformatics to direct treatment.
order: 4
---

When microarrays were first used to profile breast cancer, it became starkly apparent that there were enormous gene expression differences from sample to sample. In the two decades following the first breast cancer microarrays, the research community has learned how to characterize the differences between the various tumors. With the vast amount of gene expression and other genomic data that has now been collected, we have an opportunity to use this data to develop therapy.

In a proof-of-concept study, we used gene signatures to determine which pathways are active in tumors, found drugs that targeted these pathways, and then treated preclinical tumor models with combination therapy to block tumor growth. This proof-of-concept work has been successfully extended into patient-derived xenografts, where we treated tumors using combination therapy (Oncogene 2017). Our current work is centered on determining how to treat tumors that are resistant to the initial and standard-of-care therapy.
