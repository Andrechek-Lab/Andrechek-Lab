title: Identification of triple negative breast cancer drivers

With the various databases our team has assembled to examine mouse models of breast cancer, we are uniquely positioned to use this data to generate testable hypotheses. One area we have focused on is using these data to ask what drives triple negative (estrogen receptor, progesterone receptor, and HER2 negative) tumor initiation and progression. This is a critical area of research, since the majority of triple negative cancers are limited to chemotherapy. Even worse, these tumors predominantly occur in younger women and are more commonly observed in the African American and Latino populations.

In comparisons of mouse models that mimic triple negative breast cancer to models that mimic the luminal subtypes, we have uncovered a number of potential drivers and modulators of cancer. To directly test these genes for oncogenic properties we use overexpression and knockout experiments in cancer cell lines, and for the most promising candidates we then progress to the generation of new mouse models.
